### [Qwen 3.8 27B Matches GPT-5.6 Luna on AI Index](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/17/qwen-38-27b-scores-52/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Qwen 3.8 27B, an open-weights model with 27 billion parameters, has scored 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matching the maximum score of GPT-5.6 Luna and trailing just one point behind GLM-5.2 \(753B parameters\) and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 \(1.7T parameters\). The result is notable because the Qwen model achieves parity with much larger proprietary systems, with GPT-5.6 Luna&\#x27;s parameter count undisclosed but presumed substantially higher than 27B. Simon Willison, who reported the benchmark on August 17, 2026, described Qwen 3.8 27B as &quot;a truly astonishing model,&quot; highlighting the efficiency breakthrough. This development signals that smaller open models can now compete with frontier-scale systems on standardized intelligence benchmarks, a significant shift in LLM scaling economics.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 17, 23:58

**「Background」** The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is a standardized benchmark that measures the overall intelligence of large language models, allowing direct comparison across different architectures and sizes. Historically, higher scores on such indices have been strongly correlated with larger parameter counts, making it rare for a 27B-parameter model to match or approach the performance of models with hundreds of billions or trillions of parameters.

**「Impact」** For AI/ML practitioners and organizations deploying open models, this result suggests that Qwen 3.8 27B can deliver frontier-level intelligence at a fraction of the computational cost of models like GLM-5.2 or DeepSeek V4 Pro, potentially enabling on-premises or edge deployments that were previously infeasible. The one-point gap to the larger models indicates that efficiency gains are real but not yet complete, so users should verify performance on their specific tasks before assuming full parity.
