### [Nextcloud Office Desktop app sparks fork and security debate](https://nextcloud.com/blog/euro-office-comes-to-your-desktop-with-the-nextcloud-office-desktop-app/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Nextcloud has announced the Nextcloud Office Desktop app, bringing its office software to desktop users. Hacker News commenters described the app as a controversial fork of ONLYOFFICE and debated whether development by a consortium of companies is reassuring or bureaucratic. One commenter said the app appears to use an embedded Chromium Embedded Framework for its interface with a C++ core, and praised the spreadsheet experience. Another raised a security concern, pointing to 46 security-related fixes listed for ONLYOFFICE DocumentServer 10.0.0 and saying that at first sight some or all appeared missing from the Euro-Office DocumentServer fork.

hackernews · e12e · Nov 26, 11:50 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50654289)

**「Background」** Euro-Office is a free and open-source office suite and collaboration platform with editors for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs; it is a fork of the more restrictively licensed ONLYOFFICE. Nextcloud backs Euro-Office, and the project is now offered on desktops as Nextcloud Office Desktop, with turnkey Nextcloud integration through a dedicated app that is itself a fork of the ONLYOFFICE app for Nextcloud. The fork lineage matters because upstream ONLYOFFICE 10.0, released in October 2026, included more than 40 security fixes across its online editors and desktop app.

**「Impact」** For organizations considering the desktop app, the open question is whether the Euro-Office fork tracks upstream ONLYOFFICE security fixes, which would affect adoption decisions.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters were split: some welcomed the app&\#x27;s performance and the arrival of alternatives to OpenOffice, while others worried about the ONLYOFFICE fork&\#x27;s lineage, consortium governance, and missing upstream security fixes. Practical feedback was positive on the spreadsheet experience, though the app&\#x27;s apparent reliance on CEF was noted.
